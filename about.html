About Multitech Solution

Pioneering Progress Through Technology

Our Journey Since 2000

Established in April 2000, Multitech Solutions is a professionally managed, family-owned group. With over 25 years of rich experience in varied business fields, we were founded to conduct agency business for IT products and supplies in the Power Sector, our core strength.

We embarked upon this journey with a commitment to create significant value for our customers and achieve success in all our endeavors, armed with three generations of wisdom and 25 years of business acumen.

In these eventful years, we've gathered rich experience, built strong relationships, and earned goodwill. We now have clarity on where we want to be in the next 10 years, which forms the basis of our future growth.

Our Vision as a Corporate Advisor

Multitech Solutions is a Corporate Advisory Entity assisting our Principals and Clients to achieve their desired business objectives and economic performance by introducing new Technology, IT solutions and services, strategic planning, timely market intelligence, new business opportunities and relationship management.

In today's challenging business environment and increasing international competitiveness, valid market intelligence allows our customers to act quickly with increased insight, efficiency and flexibility and gain a competitive advantage for accelerated business growth.

A Modern Technology Provider

As a Technology Solution provider we continuously keep ourselves updated with the innovative technological advancements happening in the areas of interest of our offerings and introduce these new innovative technology and methods to our customers for keeping ahead of competition.

Multitech Solutions, specializes in helping you take your entire Office environment directly to business effectiveness by the most efficient & shortest route. We provide enterprise-wide e-business and IT infrastructure solutions that work for you - and keep working for you with ongoing support, education, training and systems monitoring services to keep you competitive.
